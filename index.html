FAQ

Can I apply to more than one program?

Yes, applicants may apply to multiple programs with separate applications.

Is the portfolio required?

Yes, a portfolio is required for all BFA and MFA applications.

Are international students accepted?

Yes, Yale School of Art welcomes applications from international students.
